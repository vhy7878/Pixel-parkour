Pixel Parkour!

A tiny, friendly obstacle course. Jump across the gaps, grab all the coins you can, and touch the golden flag.

Keyboard: ← → or A/D to move · Space / ↑ to jump
Goal: Reach the flag at the end. Falling sends you back to a checkpoint.
